## About Us

**HydroSophy** is a non-profit research collective dedicated to building bridges between **artificial intelligence** and the **natural sciences**. Inspired by the Greek roots *Hydro* (water) and *Sophia* (wisdom), we believe that knowledge, like water, should flow freely — nourishing discovery, dissolving disciplinary boundaries, and shaping the contours of tomorrow's science.

We design rigorous benchmarks, develop multimodal foundation models, and release open scientific datasets to help the global community evaluate, understand, and advance AI for Science.

---

## Research Focus

### AI for Science
- **Molecular Design & Drug Discovery** — structure-level molecular editing, toxicity repair, property optimization
- **Cheminformatics & Structural Biology** — bridging chemistry, biology and computational intelligence
- **Scientific Foundation Models** — multimodal reasoning across SMILES, structures, spectra, and literature

### Multimodal Intelligence
- **Vision–Language–Molecule Models** — joint understanding of molecular graphs, 2D depictions and natural language
- **Scientific Document Understanding** — extracting structured insight from heterogeneous scientific corpora
- **Cross-modal Knowledge Fusion** — unifying symbolic, visual and textual representations of science

### Benchmark & Evaluation
- **Rigorous Scientific Benchmarks** — reproducible evaluation protocols for chemistry, biology and beyond
- **High-Quality Curated Datasets** — expert-annotated, mechanism-aware, multimodal data releases
- **Open Evaluation Frameworks** — transparent, extensible toolkits for the AI4Science community

## Featured Projects

### [ToxiMol](https://github.com/HydroSophy/ToxiMol) &nbsp;·&nbsp; *KDD 2026 Oral*
> **Breaking Bad Molecules: Are MLLMs Ready for Structure-Level Molecular Detoxification?**

The first comprehensive benchmark for **molecular toxicity repair** tailored to general-purpose Multimodal Large Language Models — 660 toxic molecules, 11 repair tasks, multimodal inputs (SMILES + 2D structures), and the companion **ToxiEval** evaluation framework.

[![Paper](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2506.10912-b31b1b.svg?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10912)

[![Code](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-GitHub-0B2A4A.svg?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/HydroSophy/ToxiMol)

### [ToxiEval-ZKP](https://github.com/HydroSophy/ToxiEval-ZKP)
> **Zero-Knowledge Proof extensions for the ToxiEval framework.**

Verifiable, privacy-preserving evaluation of molecular detoxification pipelines.
